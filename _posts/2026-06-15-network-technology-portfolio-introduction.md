Welcome to my Network Technology Portfolio.

This blog documents the hands-on labs and projects I complete as part of my Network+ studies. The goal of this portfolio is to showcase my growth in networking concepts, troubleshooting, network design, security, and system administration.

Each lab entry will include:

- The objective of the lab
- Tools and technologies used
- Configuration steps
- Challenges encountered
- Troubleshooting performed
- Lessons learned

By maintaining this portfolio, I can track my progress and demonstrate practical networking skills through real lab experience.
